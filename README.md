# Learn Chinese: free tools and resources

A short, curated list of free tools for learning Mandarin Chinese, grouped by what you want to do. Every entry works in a browser without paying. Pull requests that add a free tool are welcome.

Kept by [Mandarin The Wild](https://mandarinthewild.com), so our own free tools are on the list and marked as ours.

## Pronunciation and tones

- [Tone pairs](https://mandarinthewild.com/tone-pairs) (ours): the 20 two-syllable tone pairs, each a real word with its pitch drawn on it, to hear in a female or male voice.
- [Tone practice](https://mandarinthewild.com/tone-practice) (ours): hear a real word, say it, and see your pitch drawn over a native speaker’s. Your voice stays on your device.
- [Tone sandhi checker](https://mandarinthewild.com/tone-sandhi) (ours): paste Mandarin and see every tone that changes when it’s said (你好 ní hǎo, 一个 yí gè, 不是 bú shì), with the rule.
- [Forvo](https://forvo.com/): words recorded by native speakers.
- [Hacking Chinese on tones](https://www.hackingchinese.com/tag/tones/): articles on hearing and saying the tones.

## Pinyin

- [Pinyin chart](https://mandarinthewild.com/pinyin-chart) (ours): every Mandarin syllable, each with its four tones to hear in a female or male voice.
- [Pinyin trainer](https://mandarinthewild.com/pinyin-trainer) and [pinyin test](https://mandarinthewild.com/pinyin-test) (ours): hear a real word and name its tones or spell it; the test gives a score with the tones you hear best and worst.
- [Pinyin converter](https://mandarinthewild.com/pinyin-converter) (ours): paste Chinese and get pinyin above every character, word by word, with the right reading for words like 行 and 长.
- [Pinyin to Chinese](https://mandarinthewild.com/pinyin-to-chinese) (ours): type pinyin, with tones or without, and pick each word in characters with its meaning, without installing a keyboard.
- [How to type tone marks](https://mandarinthewild.com/pinyin-tone-marks) (ours).

## Characters

- [Stroke order](https://mandarinthewild.com/stroke-order) (ours): thousands of characters animated stroke by stroke, with a printable practice sheet.
- [Most common Chinese characters](https://mandarinthewild.com/most-common-chinese-characters) (ours): counted in real Chinese web text, with pinyin and meaning.
- [Chinese radicals](https://mandarinthewild.com/chinese-radicals) (ours): the 214 radicals with their side forms, ranked by how many HSK characters use each.
- [Simplified to traditional](https://mandarinthewild.com/simplified-to-traditional) and [traditional to simplified](https://mandarinthewild.com/traditional-to-simplified) (ours): converted word by word, so 头发 becomes 頭髮 and 发现 發現.
- [Hanzi Writer](https://hanziwriter.org/): an open-source library for stroke animations and quizzes.
- [Arch Chinese](https://www.archchinese.com/): character worksheets you can print.

## Dictionaries and pop-up readers

- [Mandarin The Wild](https://mandarinthewild.com/extension) (ours): a browser extension that shows the one meaning a word has in the sentence you are reading, on any page or YouTube caption, with its pinyin.
- [Zhongwen](https://github.com/cschiller/zhongwen): the classic open-source pop-up dictionary for Chrome.
- [MDBG](https://www.mdbg.net/chinese/dictionary): a free dictionary website.
- [Pleco](https://www.pleco.com/): the phone dictionary most learners start with. The basic app is free.

## Graded reading

- [Mandarin The Wild Library](https://mandarinthewild.com/library) (ours): free original stories and articles with audio, by topic, series and HSK level.
- [Text level checker](https://mandarinthewild.com/text-level) (ours): paste any Chinese text and see its HSK level and the words to learn before you read it.
- [Hacking Chinese Resources](https://challenges.hackingchinese.com/resources): a large, searchable index of learner resources.

## Video and subtitles

- [Pinyin on YouTube subtitles](https://mandarinthewild.com/guides/youtube-pinyin-subtitles) (ours): a comparison of the ways to get it.
- [Language Reactor](https://www.languagereactor.com/): dual subtitles on Netflix and YouTube.

## Vocabulary and HSK

- [HSK levels explained](https://mandarinthewild.com/hsk-levels) (ours): the words and characters at each level of the 2025 syllabus, with full lists.
- [Measure words](https://mandarinthewild.com/measure-words) (ours): the measure word for any noun.
- [Level test](https://mandarinthewild.com/level-test) (ours): ten real sentences; tap the words you don’t know and get your HSK level in about two minutes.
- [Anki](https://apps.ankiweb.net/): free spaced-repetition flashcards.
